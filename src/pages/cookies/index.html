Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until deleted) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

2. How We Use Cookies

LBR InfoSolutions uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They include:

  • Cookie consent preference — Remembers your cookie consent choice (stored in localStorage)
  • Session management — Maintains basic website functionality during your visit

These cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for the website to operate.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous statistical data. This information helps us improve site performance and user experience. Analytics cookies may include:

  • Pages visited and navigation paths
  • Time spent on each page
  • Bounce rate and exit pages
  • Traffic sources and referral information
  • Device and browser information

Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your preferences (language, region) and providing improved features.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. Third-party services we may use include:

  • Google Analytics — For website usage analytics
  • Google Fonts — For typography rendering

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies which govern their use of your information.

4. Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner offering the following options:

  • Allow All — Accepts all cookies including analytics and functional
  • Necessary Only — Accepts only strictly necessary cookies
  • Reject Optional — Rejects all non-essential cookies

You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this website, which will cause the consent banner to appear again on your next visit.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block all or third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Please note that blocking all cookies may impact website functionality and your browsing experience.

6. Local Storage

In addition to cookies, we use browser localStorage to store your cookie consent preference. Local storage data persists until explicitly cleared and is not transmitted to our servers with each request.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our data practices. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.

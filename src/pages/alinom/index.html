Overview

Alinom is a comprehensive IT monitoring platform designed for managing complex enterprise infrastructure, telecommunication networks, and engineering systems at scale. It provides all-in-one monitoring capabilities with multi-technology support, advanced visualization, and open integration.

Unlike traditional monitoring tools that charge per-node or per-metric, Alinom offers enterprise-grade capabilities without licensing constraints — making comprehensive observability accessible to organizations of all sizes while maintaining the depth and reliability demanded by large-scale operations.
